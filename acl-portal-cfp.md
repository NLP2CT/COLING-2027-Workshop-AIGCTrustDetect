# AIGCTrustDetect 2027: The 1st Workshop on AI-Generated Content Detection and Trustworthy Governance

## Call for Papers

**Co-located with COLING 2027, Macao, China**  
**Conference:** 9-14 May 2027  
**Workshop:** One day in May 2027; the exact date will be confirmed  
**Paper submission deadline:** 10 January 2027 (Anywhere on Earth, UTC-12)  
**Full CFP:** https://nlp2ct.github.io/COLING-2027-Workshop-AIGCTrustDetect/call-for-papers [1]

AIGCTrustDetect 2027 invites original, unpublished research on secure, explainable and robust detection and governance of AI-generated content. The workshop especially welcomes research that moves beyond binary detection toward fine-grained, cross-lingual and content-level evaluation, with clear potential for generalization and cost-effective real-world deployment.

## Topics

Topics include, but are not limited to:

- **Reliable and explainable detection.** Detection methods for AI-generated text: zero-shot and supervised detectors, watermarking and provenance signals, stylometric and statistical features; interpretability, calibration and uncertainty estimation; attribution to specific models or generators; evaluation protocols, benchmarking and error analysis; decisions that humans can verify and contest.
- **Detection across modalities.** AI-generated content detection beyond text: speech, image, video and multimodal content, including deepfakes and synthetic media, and the transfer of detection techniques across modalities.
- **Detection in complex generation scenarios.** Mixed human-AI authorship and LLM-refined text; paraphrasing, translation and other obfuscation; adversarial attacks on detectors and deliberate evasion; robustness and generalization across models, domains, prompts and decoding strategies; real-time and large-scale detection systems.
- **Detection and governance in agentic workflows.** Content produced by LLM agents and tool-using systems: provenance and audit trails across multi-step generation, detection and attribution of AI-generated code, reliability of agent-written reports, and human oversight of automated content pipelines.
- **Cross-lingual generalization.** Multilingual and cross-lingual detection; transfer and low-cost adaptation to low-resource languages; language-agnostic signals; multilingual datasets and benchmarks.
- **Linguistic perspectives on AI-generated text.** Syntactic, semantic, discourse and pragmatic signatures of machine writing; corpus-based and stylometric comparison of human and machine text; register, genre and stylistic variation across languages; linguistic typology and its implications for detection; and human perception of AI-generated language.
- **Hallucination, reliability assessment and factuality verification.** Whether generated content is accurate, supported and proportionate: hallucination detection, evidence grounding and claim verification, evidence-statement mismatch and overclaim, fake news and misinformation, reliability assessment of generated text, and grounding and citation fidelity in retrieval-augmented generation.
- **AI assistance in scholarly writing and peer review.** Detection and disclosure of AI-generated manuscripts and reviews, reliability of LLM-as-a-judge evaluation, and governance of AI assistance across the research pipeline.
- **Hierarchical and differentiated governance.** Governance itself as a research problem: tiered frameworks and new tasks that formalize proportionate response across settings with different stakes, from education to journalism to content platforms; disclosure, provenance standards and policy; fairness and disparate impact of detectors, safeguards against false accusation; ethical, legal and societal implications of AI-generated content.
- **Synthetic data and training-set governance.** Detection and labeling of AI-generated content in training corpora, reliability of synthetic data, contamination and deduplication, and implications of recursive training for future detectors.
- **Datasets, resources and tooling.** Corpora, benchmarks, evaluation tools and open-source infrastructure supporting any of the directions above.

## Shared Tasks

The workshop hosts three shared tasks, and registration is now open:

- **Track 1: Multilingual LLM-Generated Text Detection.** Participants will classify text as human-written, LLM-generated or LLM-refined across Arabic, Russian, Chinese, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and English.
- **Track 2: Multilingual LLM-Generated Archaic Text Detection.** Participants will detect LLM-generated text in Latin, Ancient Greek, Old Arabic, Sanskrit and Classical Chinese.
- **Track 3: LLM-Generated Text Reliability Assessment.** Participants will identify overstated claims, unsupported arguments and mismatches between statements and their supporting evidence in English text.

**Shared-task details:** https://nlp2ct.github.io/COLING-2027-Workshop-AIGCTrustDetect/shared-tasks [2]

Registration is available through Google Form [3]. Users in mainland China who cannot access Google Forms may use the alternative registration form [4].

## Submissions

- **Paper categories and page limits:** Long papers: up to 8 pages of content; short papers: up to 4 pages of content; shared-task system papers: up to 4 pages of content, for participating teams.
- **References:** All paper types allow unlimited reference pages.
- **Formatting:** PDF using the [official ACL template](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-style-files) in review mode, following the ACL two-column A4 format, the [ACL/ARR requirements](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp), and the [ACL formatting guidelines](https://acl-org.github.io/ACLPUB/formatting.html).
- **Anonymity and review:** Double-anonymous review. Anonymize the paper, supplementary materials, and linked artifacts in accordance with the [ARR policy](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp#instructions-for-two-way-anonymized-review).
- **ARR commitments:** Commitments must include completed ARR reviews and a meta-review and satisfy the current ARR commitment requirements. Papers must no longer be under ARR review. Authors submitting to ARR must also follow its current profile, reviewing-service, and submission policies.
- **Required section and checklist:** Include a Limitations section and complete the [Responsible NLP Research Checklist](https://aclrollingreview.org/responsibleNLPresearch/) with the submission.
- **Publication options:** Archival and non-archival. Only archival papers will be included in the workshop proceedings.
- **Originality:** Archival submissions must report original, unpublished work that is not under review elsewhere. The non-archival option permits dual submission; authors must comply with the policies of any other venue involved.

Each paper will receive at least two reviews from Program Committee members, and final acceptance decisions will be made by Senior Program Committee members.

Direct submissions will be handled through OpenReview under the COLING 2027 Workshop venue:
https://openreview.net/group?id=coling-iccl.org/COLING/2027/Workshop [5]

We also welcome ARR commitments with completed reviews and a meta-review. The workshop-specific direct submission and ARR commitment links will be provided under this venue and announced on the workshop website.

### Non-Archival Option

Workshops are traditionally archival. To allow dual submission of work, we also include a non-archival track. If accepted, these submissions will still participate and present their work in the workshop. A reference to the paper will be hosted on the workshop website if desired, but the paper will not be included in the official workshop proceedings. Please submit through OpenReview and indicate that this is a cross-submission at the bottom of the submission form. You can also skip this step and inform us of your non-archival preference after the reviews.

Presentation and attendance will be hybrid, with in-person and online participation. Presentation formats will be announced with acceptance decisions. All accepted submissions, whether archival or non-archival, will be presented at the workshop. Only archival papers will be included in the workshop proceedings.
